Number Quest

A number path adventure! The tiles 1 to 25 are shuffled on the board. Tap them in order from 1 to 25 to light the path to the star. Each correct tile scores 10 points, but a wrong tap costs a life — you have 3.

Score: 0 Lives: ❤❤❤ Time: 0:00

Tap tile 1 to start!

Controls: tap or click a tile — or use Tab to move between tiles and Enter to tap one. Tap the tiles in order from 1 to 25. A correct tile lights up teal and scores 10 points. A wrong tile flashes red and costs 1 of your 3 lives. Find all 25 tiles to reach the star. The timer starts on your first correct tile and counts up — faster is better!
