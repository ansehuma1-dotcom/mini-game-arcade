Rocket Launch

Blast off into space! Hold to thrust, steer around the asteroids and grab the stars. Reach 500 metres high to win — you have 3 lives.

Score: 0 Altitude: 0m Lives: 3

Hold Space, hold the Thrust button, or hold your finger on the game to lift off!

Controls: hold Space, hold the Thrust button, or hold your finger on the game to thrust upward. Steer left and right with the arrow keys or by dragging. Dodge the asteroids, grab stars for 10 points each, and reach 500 metres to win. An asteroid hit costs a life — you have 3.
