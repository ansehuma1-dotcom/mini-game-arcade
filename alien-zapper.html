Alien Zapper

Aliens are descending! Move your ship, zap them all, and don't let any reach the bottom. You have 3 lives.

Score: 0 Lives: 3 Wave: 1

Zap every alien before it lands!

Controls: move with the ← → arrow keys, drag on the game area, or hold the ◀ ▶ buttons. Press Space, tap Fire, or tap the game area to shoot. Each alien you zap is worth 10 points. An alien that reaches the bottom costs 1 life — lose all 3 lives and the game ends. Clear a wave to face a faster next one.
