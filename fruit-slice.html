Fruit Slice

Swipe across fruit to slice it. Build a combo by slicing quickly. Do not slice a bomb. Missing a fruit costs a life. You have 60 seconds and 3 lives.

Score: 0 Combo: x1 Time: 60s Lives: ❤️❤️❤️ Best: 0

Ready to slice?

Swipe with your mouse or finger. Slice fruit, avoid bombs, don't let fruit fall.

Sound is off until you start. Use the sound button below to turn it on or off.

Controls: mouse drag or touch swipe across the game area. Keyboard: press S to start, P to pause, R to restart. While you are swiping inside the game, the page will not scroll; normal scrolling works outside the game.
