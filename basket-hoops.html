Basket Hoops

The hoop keeps moving — can you keep scoring? Flick the ball with a drag and let it fly. Each basket is 10 points, and 3 in a row earns a bonus. You have 60 seconds!

Score: 0 Time: 60 Streak: 0

Drag from the ball to aim, then let go to shoot!

Controls: drag from the ball and flick in the direction you want to throw — the dotted line shows the arc — then let go to shoot. Or use the keyboard: Left/Right arrows move the ball's launch spot, Up/Down set the power, and Space shoots. The hoop slides side to side, so time your shot. Each basket is 10 points, 3 in a row earns a 5-point streak bonus, and you have 60 seconds.
