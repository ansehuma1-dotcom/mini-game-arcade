Farm Merge

Plant sprouts, watch them grow, and merge matching crops into bigger ones! Tap an empty plot to plant, tap a crop to select it, and tap a matching crop to merge. Harvest grown crops for coins.

Coins: 0 Crops merged: 0

Tap an empty plot to plant your first sprout!

Controls: tap an empty plot to plant a sprout. Crops grow on their own in a few seconds. Tap a crop to select it, tap a matching crop to merge them into a bigger crop, and tap a selected crop again when it is fully grown to harvest it for coins. Sprout, carrot, pumpkin, sunflower, then golden melon — bigger crops are worth more coins.
