Helicopter Rescue

Hikers are stranded on the city rooftops! Fly your rescue helicopter, hover over a hiker to lift them aboard, then drop them safely at the helipad. Rescue 5 hikers to win — but watch out for the birds!

Rescued: 0/5 Score: 0 Lives: 3

Rescue 5 stranded hikers! Hover over a hiker to lift them aboard, then drop them at the helipad (H).

How to play

Fly the helicopter with the arrow keys or the Up and Down buttons. Hover over a waving hiker for about a second to winch them aboard — you can carry one hiker at a time. Then fly to the helipad marked H on the left rooftop and hover there to drop them off safely. Each rescue scores 50 points. Birds cross the sky: hitting one costs a life. You have 3 lives. Rescue 5 hikers to win!

Controls: ArrowUp / W or the Up button to climb, ArrowDown / S or the Down button to descend, ArrowLeft / A and ArrowRight / D to fly left and right, or drag the helicopter on the game area with your finger or mouse. Hold still over a hiker to pick them up, then hold still over the helipad to drop them off.
