Bubble Blast

Bubbles are hanging from the top! Aim the cannon and shoot to match 3 or more of the same colour. Pop them all before you run out of shots — or before they reach the red line.

Score: 0 Shots left: 30

Aim with your mouse or finger, then click, tap or press Space to shoot!

Controls: move your mouse or finger over the game to aim the cannon, then click or tap to shoot (or use the Left/Right arrow keys to aim and Space to shoot). Match 3 or more bubbles of the same colour to pop them for 10 points each. Bubbles cut off from the top fall away for 5 points each. Every 5 shots a new row of bubbles drops in. Clear all the bubbles to win — if they cross the red line, or you run out of shots, you lose.
