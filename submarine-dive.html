Submarine Dive

Dive into the deep sea! Steer your little submarine up and down, dodge the drifting mines and collect pearls. Find 10 pearls in 60 seconds to win!

Score: 0 Pearls: 0/10 Time left: 60s Lives: 3

Use the arrow keys or drag on the game to steer. Collect 10 pearls!

Controls: move up and down with the Up/Down arrow keys (or W/S), or drag on the game. Dodge the mines — 3 hits end the dive. Collect pearls for 10 points each and find all 10 pearls to win. You have 60 seconds.
