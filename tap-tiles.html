Tap Tiles

Tiles fall down 4 lanes. Tap a tile while it is in the glowing hit zone near the bottom. 3 misses and the game ends.

Score: 0 Best: 0 Missed: 0/3

Press Start, then tap the tiles in the hit zone.

Controls: tap or click a tile in the hit zone, or press D F J K (or 1 to 4) for lanes 1 to 4.
