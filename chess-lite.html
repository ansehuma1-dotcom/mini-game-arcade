Chess Lite

A friendly game of chess against the computer! Move your white pieces, capture the black ones, and checkmate the black king. The computer answers every move — take your time, there is no clock.

Turn: White Captured: —

Your turn — tap a white piece to move.

Controls: click or tap one of your white pieces to see where it can move, then click a highlighted square to move there. The computer plays black and answers after a short pause. Lite rules: no castling or en passant, and pawns promote to queen.
