City Builder

Start a tiny town from empty plots! Build houses, shops and parks, earn coins over time and reach the target to grow. 3 levels with bigger targets.

Coins: 50 Target: 150 Level: 1/3

Pick a building, then tap an empty plot to build it!

Controls: pick House, Shop or Park, then tap an empty plot to build it. Buildings earn coins every couple of seconds — houses are cheap, shops earn the most. Reach the coin target to move to the next level. Keyboard: 1 = House, 2 = Shop, 3 = Park.
