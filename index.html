🍉 Fruit Slice

Slice flying fruit, build a combo, and avoid the bombs. 60 seconds, 3 lives, mouse or touch.

Play Fruit Slice

How to play: swipe across a fruit to slice it. Do not slice a bomb. Missing a fruit costs a life.

🧠 Memory Match

Flip cards and find all 8 pairs. Timer, move counter, restart, and a win screen — mouse, touch, and keyboard.

Play Memory Match

How to play: flip two cards at a time. Matching pairs stay face up.
