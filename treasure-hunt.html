Treasure Hunt

Three treasures are buried on this island map! You have 15 digs to find them all. Each dig gives you a hot, warm or cold clue — follow the clues to the gold!

Treasures found: 0/3 Digs left: 15

Tap a square to dig for treasure!

Controls: tap a square to dig. You have 15 digs to find 3 hidden treasures. After each dig you get a clue: hot 🔥 means a treasure is right next to you, warm ☀️ means it is close, cold ❄️ means it is far away. Arrow keys move the shovel, Enter or Space digs.
