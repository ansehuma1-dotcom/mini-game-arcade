Tower Siege

Enemies are marching on your castle! Build towers on the marked spots to stop them. Earn gold from every enemy you stop and survive all 10 waves to win.

Gold: 150 Wave: 0/10 Castle HP: 10

Tap a build spot, then pick a tower — then press Next Wave!

Controls: tap a dashed build spot on the map, then tap a tower button to build it there. Arrow towers shoot fast, Frost towers slow enemies, Cannon towers hit hard. Tap one of your towers and press Upgrade to make it stronger (+1 damage, up to level 3). Enemies you stop earn 8 gold. If 10 enemies reach the castle, you lose — survive all 10 waves to win.
