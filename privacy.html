Privacy Policy

Mini Game Arcade is a static website. It does not require an account, and it does not collect names, email addresses, or payment details to play a game.

Your best score in Fruit Slice is stored only in your own browser (local storage) on your device. Clearing your browser data removes it. We do not receive that score.

The hosting provider (Vercel) may log basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, as described in its own privacy policy.

No advertising code is currently placed on this site. If advertising is added in future, this policy will be updated before it goes live to accurately describe any advertising technology used, including cookies from advertising partners.
