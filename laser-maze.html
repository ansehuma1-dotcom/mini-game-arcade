Laser Maze

A laser puzzle! Rotate the mirrors to bend the red beam around the walls and hit the golden target. Three levels, no timer — take your time and think it through.

Level: 1/3 Moves: 0

Tap a mirror to rotate it and guide the beam to the target!

Controls: tap or click a mirror to rotate it. The red laser fires from the left — bend it around the walls with the mirrors until it hits the golden target. Solve all 3 levels in as few moves as you can. Arrow keys move the selection, Enter or Space rotates the selected mirror.
