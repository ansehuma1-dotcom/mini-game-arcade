Crossword Mini

A pocket-size crossword! Five everyday words cross each other in a 5×5 grid. Read the clues, fill in the letters, and tap Check when you think you have got them all.

Score: 0/5

Tap a square, then type a letter — or use the A–Z buttons below.

Across

    Down

      Controls: tap a square to select it, then type a letter on your keyboard or tap an A–Z button. Tap the same square again to switch between its Across and Down word. Arrow keys move around the grid, Backspace clears a letter. Fill in all five words from the clues, then tap Check — correct words turn green, wrong letters turn red.
