Jungle Snake

Steer the jungle snake through the vines! Eat red fruit to grow and score, grab golden fruit for a bonus — but dodge the rocks, the walls and your own tail.

Eat fruit, grow longer, and dodge the rocks in this jungle snake game — keyboard, swipe, or on-screen arrows.

Score: 0 Best: 0

Press Play or steer with an arrow key to start!

How to play: steer with arrow keys, WASD, swipe, or the on-screen arrows. Eat red fruit for 10 points and golden fruit for 50. Do not hit a rock, a wall, or your own tail.
