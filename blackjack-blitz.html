Blackjack Blitz

Classic blackjack against the dealer! You start with 100 play chips and bet 10 chips a round. Beat the dealer to 21 without going over — a blackjack pays 3 to 2. Chips are play points only; no real money is involved.

Chips: 100 Bet: 10

Tap Deal to start a round — 10 chips a round, blackjack pays 3 to 2!

Controls: tap Deal to start a round (10 chips), then Hit to take a card or Stand to let the dealer play. The dealer draws to 17. Aces count 11 or 1, whichever is better. Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Chips are play points only — no real money. Keyboard: D to deal, H to hit, S to stand, R to restart.
