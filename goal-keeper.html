Goal Keeper

You are the goalkeeper! The shooter is lining up 10 shots. Watch, dive left, centre or right, and stop as many as you can. Each save is 10 points!

Saves: 0 Shots left: 10

Watch the shooter — dive left, centre or right!

Controls: watch the shooter wind up, then dive before the ball arrives — tap the left, centre or right side of the goal, use the dive buttons, or press the Left / Down / Right arrow keys (Down is centre). Each save is 10 points. You face 10 shots — save as many as you can!
