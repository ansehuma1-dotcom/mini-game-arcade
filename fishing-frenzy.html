Fishing Frenzy

The pond is full of fish! Move your hook, drop it at the right moment, and reel in a catch. Normal fish are worth 10 points, golden fish 30. You have 60 seconds — how many can you catch?

Score: 0 Time left: 60 Caught: 0

Move the hook and drop it on a fish!

Controls: move the hook with the Left/Right arrow keys, drag it on the game area, or use the Left/Right buttons. Press Space, tap the Drop button, or tap the water to lower the hook — it grabs the first fish it touches and reels it in. Normal fish are worth 10, golden fish 30.
