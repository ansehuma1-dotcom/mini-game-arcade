Checkers Clash

A friendly game of checkers against the computer! Jump the black pieces, crown your kings on the far row, and capture them all. The computer answers every move — take your time.

Turn: You Pieces: Red 12 — Black 12

Your turn — tap a red piece to see where it can move.

Controls: click or tap one of your red pieces to see where it can move, then click a highlighted square. Jump over a black piece to capture it — if the same piece can jump again, keep jumping! Reach the far row to crown a king, which moves both ways. Capture all the black pieces, or block them so they cannot move, to win. Press R to restart.
