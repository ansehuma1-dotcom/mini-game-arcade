Penguin Slide

Your penguin slides on its own! Jump the cracks, slide under the icicles, and go as far as you can. 3 hits and the slide is over.

Score: 0 Distance: 0m Hits: 0/3

Tap or press Space to jump!

Controls: press Space, Up or tap the game to jump over ice cracks. Press Down or hold the Slide button to slide under icicles. You can take 3 hits — the slide gets faster the longer you go.
