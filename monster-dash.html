Monster Dash

Your monster runs on its own — you just jump! Time your jumps to clear the gaps and the spiky rocks, and grab the stars. The further you run, the faster it gets.

Score: 0 Distance: 0 m Stars: 0 Lives: ♥♥♥

Press Space or tap Jump to start running!

Controls: press Space or the Up arrow, tap the game, or use the Jump button to jump over the gaps and spiky rocks. Collect floating stars for 10 points each. You have 3 hearts — falling in a gap or hitting a rock costs one heart. The dash gets faster the further you run.
