Bridge Builder

A walker needs to cross the river! Drop wooden planks one at a time to build a bridge. You have a limited number of planks — finish the bridge before they run out. 3 levels, wider gaps each time.

Level: 1/3 Planks left: 5 Score: 0

Tap Build Plank, tap the river, or press Space to drop a plank!

Controls: tap Build Plank or press Space to drop a plank at the end of the bridge. Tapping the river drops a plank where you tap — a plank that misses the end of the bridge sinks and is wasted. Finish the bridge before your planks run out so the walker can cross. Unused planks are worth 10 points each.
