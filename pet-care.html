Pet Care

Meet your new best friend! Feed your pet, play with it and let it sleep. Keep Hunger, Happiness and Energy high to earn coins and care points — but if any meter hits zero, your pet gets sad!

Your pet is happy and ready! Tap Feed, Play or Sleep to look after it.

How to play: tap Feed to raise Hunger, Play to raise Happiness, and Sleep to raise Energy. Each care action costs 5 coins. Coins trickle in while your pet is healthy, and you earn 1 care point every 2 seconds while all meters are above 20. If any meter hits 0 your pet gets sad and points and coins stop — raise that meter to cheer it up again!
